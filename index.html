Txtura (com.txtura) is a fast, ad-free Default SMS and MMS messaging application for Android built with Jetpack Compose. Your messages stay 100% private on your device with SQLCipher AES-256 local encryption, zero analytics, and optional end-to-end encrypted Google Drive backup.

Light, Dark, AMOLED, and Auto modes paired with 47 background textures (including 24 live animated effects) and per-contact chat wallpapers.

Local-First Security & Ghost Chat

SQLCipher AES-256 database encryption, biometric/PIN app lock, salted PBKDF2 Ghost Chat hidden conversations, EXIF stripping, and on-device phishing & OTP protection.

End-to-End Encrypted Google Drive Backup

Optionally back up and restore your messages and settings to your own hidden Google Drive app folder (drive.appdata), encrypted locally with AES-256-GCM + PBKDF2 before upload.

4 Adaptive Widgets & Smart Replies

Includes Messages, Unread, Single Contact, and Contacts Grid widgets, Android Auto & smartwatch MessagingStyle notifications, Dual SIM support, and reliable scheduled messages.

Google Drive API & Privacy Transparency

Google Drive integration in Txtura is completely optional and disabled by default. When explicitly enabled by the user, Txtura requests only the non-sensitive https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope to store, restore, or delete the user's own AES-256-GCM encrypted backup file in their private hidden app folder. Txtura cannot access any other Google Drive files and strictly complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

Privatnost na prvom mestu • Android SMS i MMS

Privatne, moderne SMS i MMS poruke u dizajnu tečnog stakla

Txtura (com.txtura) je brza podrazumevana SMS i MMS aplikacija za Android bez reklama i praćenja. Sve vaše poruke ostaju 100% privatne na uređaju uz lokalnu SQLCipher AES-256 enkripciju i opcioni end-to-end šifrovani Google Drive bekap.

Preuzmi na Google Play-u Politika privatnosti Uslovi korišćenja Bezbednost podataka

47 tekstura i prilagodljive teme

Svetli, tamni, AMOLED i automatski režim uz 47 ručno izrađenih tekstura (24 animirane) i posebne pozadine za svaki kontakt.

Lokalna bezbednost i Skriveni razgovori

SQLCipher AES-256 zaštita lokalne baze, biometrijsko zaključavanje, PBKDF2 zaštita PIN-a za Skrivene razgovore, uklanjanje EXIF podataka i lokalna zaštita od fišinga i OTP kodova.

End-to-end šifrovani Google Drive bekap

Opciono ručno ili automatsko čuvanje poruka i podešavanja u privatnu skrivenu fasciklu vašeg Google Drive-a (drive.appdata), šifrovano na telefonu pomoću AES-256-GCM + PBKDF2 pre slanja.

4 prilagodljiva vidžeta i Android Auto

Vidžeti Poruke, Nepročitane, Pojedinačni kontakt i Mreža kontakata, bogata MessagingStyle obaveštenja za Android Auto i pametne satove, Dual SIM podrška i pouzdano zakazivanje poruka.

Google Drive API i transparentnost privatnosti

Povezivanje sa Google Drive-om je potpuno opciono i podrazumevano isključeno. Kada ga korisnik izričito uključi, Txtura koristi isključivo neosetljivi opseg https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata radi čuvanja, vraćanja ili brisanja korisnikove AES-256-GCM šifrovane kopije u skrivenoj fascikli aplikacije. Aplikacija nema pristup drugim fajlovima na Drive-u i u potpunosti poštuje Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use).
